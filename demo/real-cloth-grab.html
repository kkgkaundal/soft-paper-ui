Grab Algorithm:

1. Find cluster: All particles within 60px of mouse
2. Calculate influence: influence = (1 - distance/radius)²
3. Apply force: dragForce = 0.12 × influence
4. Track velocity: mouseVelocity = currentPos - prevPos
5. On release: particle.velocity += mouseVelocity × influence × 0.3

Weight Distribution:

Top row: mass = 1.0 (light)
Middle: mass = 1.25 (medium)
Bottom row: mass = 1.5 (heavy)
Effect: Natural draping, heavier bottom sways more

Key Improvements:

  • Cluster grab: 60px radius captures 12-20 particles vs 1 before
  • Squared falloff: Concentrated influence (closer follows more)
  • Mass gradient: Bottom 50% heavier for natural drape
  • Velocity tracking: Smooth momentum on release
  • Softer forces: 0.12 drag (was 0.15) for ultra-smooth feel
